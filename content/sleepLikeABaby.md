This is a journal/advices of a person who is obsessed(lack of a better word) with sleep schedule.

### Things to keep in mind , if you want to have a Good Night sleep
1. Set an alaram , Mhmm not for waking up but for your bed time.
2. Data - Data - Data , Gather as much Data possible.
3. Prioritize your sleep

### Detailed Section

#### Set an alaram , Mhmm not for waking up but for your bed time.

- Generally people set the alaram when they want to wake up , yes it's an effective option but I believe 
your body should decide when to wake.
    - Few people will argue that their body would never want ( then you have to focus on the other areas as well).
- While waking up alarm is optional but an remidner/ alarm for your bed time is mandatory.
- Try to sleep at the same time and wake up at the same time.
    - I know practically not possible but half an hour here and there is fine.

#### Data - Data - Data

- If you want to improve on anything , you need data .Its like exam results .
- Have a band , that tracks sleep , I have `fitbit band` ( its the most cheapest and bit accurate).
   - you are not going to get 100% accurate anywhere 
- Monitor 
    - your total time of sleep ( i.e Deep , Light , Reem)
    - Your Resting Heart rate , this will tell you how efficient your heart is.


 #### Prioritize your sleep

 - Everything else , I literally meant everything else should be around your sleep 
 - Plan your day according that
    - meeting friends , partying , going out , travelling , working , spending time with family , all this should revolve 
     around sleep schedule not vice versa.
 - Learn to say `NO` to stuff that you don't enjoy.
