> "Desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want." — Naval Ravikant

This isn’t just a quote. It’s a mindset shift.

Naval Ravikant, a modern-day philosopher and angel investor, redefines how we think about happiness. According to him, happiness isn’t a gift—it’s a **skill**. Like fitness or coding, it’s something you can learn, refine, and master.

Let’s break it down.

---
![[monkey-with-plan5.png]]

### Don’t Take Yourself Too Seriously

- You're just a monkey with a plan, as Naval puts it.
- Most of our stress comes from attaching too much identity to every thought, role, or plan.
- Zoom out and realize: it’s all temporary. Laugh a little more.


---

### Happiness Is a Skill, Not a Trait

- You aren’t born happy. You **become** happy.
- Treat it like building muscle:
  - Try meditation, long walks, journaling.
  - Fail, adjust, repeat.
- It’s personal—your version of joy may look very different from someone else’s.

---

### Happiness Is the Absence of Lack

- When you stop feeling like something is missing, you’re already there.
- Less is more. Less desire, less regret, less distraction.
- Being present is peace.

---

### You Are Malleable, The World Is Not

- We often try to change the world to suit us.
- But in reality, **we are the ones who can change**.
- Malleability is power. Use it wisely.


---

### Cultivate Indifference to the Uncontrollable

- Can’t control it? Don’t cling to it.
- Politics, online debates, traffic—none of it should steal your peace.
- A rational person protects their inner stillness.

---

### Lower Your Ego, Embrace the Present

- Naval lowered his identity to escape the chatter.
- What that means:
  - Say no to toxic people.
  - Stop being “right” all the time.
  - Read, reflect, and be okay with silence.

---

### Presence Over Pleasure

- Happiness isn’t in scrolling, sugar, or likes.
- It’s in being fully **present**.
  - Let go of the past.
  - Stop chasing vices.
  - Observe the space between thoughts. That’s clarity.

---

### Every Desire Is a Chosen Unhappiness

- Wanting something is a decision to be unsatisfied until it arrives.
- We all fall into this trap. Awareness is the way out.
- The ultimate delusion: something “out there” will fix everything.

---

### Success Does Not Earn Happiness

- Success can’t guarantee peace.
- Peace is **happiness at rest**.
- You can have peace with less, and anxiety with more.
- It’s about satisfaction, not status.

---

### Envy Is the Enemy of Happiness

- Expectations are inherited from society. Most aren’t even yours.
- Envy compares your behind-the-scenes to someone else’s highlight reel.
- Naval reminds us: **life is a single-player game**.
  - You were forgotten before you arrived.
  - You’ll be forgotten three generations after.

---

### Build Habits That Support Happiness

- Surround yourself wisely:
  - Work: people more successful.
  - Play: people more joyful.
- Try, test, and stick to what works for *you*:
  - Vipassana, journaling, Tantra, or simply smiling more.
- Practical habits:
  - Meditate daily.
  - Reframe judgment with compassion.
  - Get sunlight and drop caffeine.
  - Say “I’m happy” aloud—it rewires your brain.
  - Move your body.
  - Reduce screen time.
  - Focus on natural serotonin boosters: tryptophan, exercise, sunlight.

---

### Mastery Is a Journey

1. Know it.
2. Understand it.
3. Explain it.
4. Feel it.
5. **Be it.**

That’s the mastery ladder—whether it’s happiness, fitness, or peace.

---

### Happiness Comes From Acceptance

- Sometimes, life doesn’t go your way—and that’s the way.
- Practice asking:
  - What’s the upside here?
  - What’s life teaching me?
  - Can I let this go?
- Accept death, too—not in a grim way, but to live more vividly.

---

### Final Thought

Naval doesn’t hand out hacks. He offers **truths**.

> Happiness isn’t found. It’s built.  
> It’s not out there. It’s in here.  
> And it’s yours—one breath at a time.
